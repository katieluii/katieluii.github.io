# Deep Thematic Synth — ADC Class State (2026-06-05)

**Scope:** NSCLC, Breast, Urothelial, Gastric/GI, CRC, PDAC, MM, NHL/DLBCL

---

## TL;DR

- **Post-Zynlonta inflection:** The loncastuximab tesirine Phase 3 DLBCL confirmatory death-rate signal (3x control, Jun 4 2026) is the most consequential ADC class event in the knowledge base to date; it has now echoed across 6 of 12 tracked indications, raising the FDA confirmatory trial bar for the entire class — but particularly hematologic and AA-pathway solid-tumour ADCs.
- **Class bifurcation confirmed:** Investment and clinical momentum are bifurcating cleanly into (a) next-gen solid-tumour ADCs with validated DXd/SN-38 payloads (T-DXd, dato-DXd, sac-gov in TNBC, EV) — actively advancing — versus (b) legacy hematologic/PBD-class AA-pathway assets (loncastuximab, belantamab) under elevated scrutiny or withdrawal risk.
- **Who is winning by target:** TROP2 is splitting within the class — sac-gov TNBC leading (ASCENT-04 1L TNBC positive), dato-DXd HR+ weakening (TROPION-Breast01 OS NS); HER2/DXd franchise (T-DXd/AZ-Daiichi) is the single most dominant ADC franchise across the landscape, now spanning breast 1L HER2+, HER2-low, urothelial pan-tumour, and CRC HER2+; Nectin-4 (EV) owns la/mUC 1L with the best HR in solid-tumour oncology in the past decade (OS HR 0.47).
- **CLDN18.2 ADC class gets its first Phase 3 anchor:** IBI343 gastric success (Innovent/Takeda $1.2B) validates CLDN18.2 as a druggable GI ADC target — but gastric-to-PDAC translation is uncertain due to stroma and lower surface density; CRC expression is even lower (~10-15%).
- **Watch flags:** Dato-DXd TROPION-Lung01 confirmatory in NSCLC is the highest single near-term regulatory risk in the solid-tumour ADC class; DREAMM-7 final OS for belantamab is the highest-urgency hematologic ADC data gap; and the absence of any ADC in gastric outside CLDN18.2 (HER2 ADC disitamab vedotin has separate urothelial momentum) marks the class frontier.

## Class Definition + Scope

### What this theme is

Antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) are biologic payloads — a tumour-antigen-targeting antibody linked to a cytotoxic payload via a cleavable or non-cleavable linker. Their value proposition is selective antigen-guided delivery of a potent cytotoxin to tumour cells, with the "bystander effect" (DXd payload diffusion to adjacent antigen-negative cells) as an additional mechanism for some new-generation agents. ADC design has evolved dramatically since the first-generation GO (gemtuzumab ozogamicin) and T-DM1 to include optimised DAR (drug-to-antibody ratio), cleavable linker chemistry, and highly potent topoisomerase-I-targeting payloads (DXd, SN-38) or MMAE microtubule-targeting.

This theme tracks **ADC as a modality class** — its safety trajectory, competitive dominance, target saturation, and the cross-indication regulatory consequences of individual asset failures. It is distinct from per-indication analysis: T-DXd's HER2 franchise is analysed across breast + NSCLC + urothelial + CRC simultaneously rather than as separate indication stories.

### What it is not

This theme does not cover antibody-only (non-conjugated) agents, IO bispecifics, or immunotoxins (e.g. oportuzumab monatox) unless they explicitly bear an ADC-like architecture. Peptide-drug conjugates (e.g. maridebart cafraglutide for obesity) share conjugate chemistry but are biologically outside ADC oncology scope and are excluded.

### Why track it now

The Zynlonta signal (Jun 2026) has created the most significant class-level regulatory event in ADC history since gemtuzumab ozogamicin's original withdrawal in 2010. This is a rare moment where a single asset failure has cross-indication consequences, requiring a class-level lens rather than indication-by-indication assessment. Simultaneously, deal activity is bifurcating (Ona Therapeutics $87M next-gen ADC Series B; Regeneron CytomX Probody expansion) — investors are routing capital away from legacy ADCs and toward next-gen designs, creating a two-tier class structure.

## Cross-Indication Dynamics

**NHL/DLBCL:** Zynlonta is effectively removed from the 3L+ DLBCL competitive landscape. The ADC segment at 3L+ DLBCL is now vacant — bispecifics (glofitamab, epcoritamab) are the primary beneficiaries. Polatuzumab vedotin (1L DLBCL, pola+R-CHP) is unaffected — it is a full approval, not AA-pathway. The class risk in DLBCL is now specifically PBD-class hematologic ADCs under AA pathway. ADCT-602 (CD22 PBD ADC, ADC Therapeutics) inherits indirect platform scrutiny.

**MM:** Belantamab mafodotin is the sole ADC in the MM knowledge base. DREAMM-10 (NDMM-TE Phase 3 confirmatory) is the principal at-risk design. The Zynlonta precedent elevates scrutiny because (a) DREAMM-10 is a confirmatory design and (b) DREAMM-7 final OS is absent — a gap the MM analyst has escalated to data_gaps rank 1. The GSK relationship to ADCC/MMAF payload (microtubule disruption, not PBD) does provide some biological differentiation from loncastuximab's PBD chemistry, but class-level regulatory caution will apply regardless of payload class. Net: belantamab re-approval is preserved, confirmatory execution risk is elevated.

**Breast:** The most active ADC battleground in the knowledge base. Three simultaneous competitive dynamics: (1) T-DXd HER2 franchise expanding 1L (DESTINY-Breast09), (2) sac-gov TNBC TROP2+IO leading (ASCENT-04), (3) dato-DXd weakening in HR+ (TROPION-Breast01 OS NS). TROP2 ADC class is bifurcating by payload: sac-gov's SN-38 payload showing durable OS in TNBC; dato-DXd's DXd payload underperforming OS vs HR+ expectations. This is a within-class payload differentiation story, not a class-level failure. Breast solid-tumour ADC confirmatory designs (ASCENT-05 adj, TROPION-Breast01/02) are monitored for the Zynlonta trigger but the ovarian mirvetuximab approval is the class's solid-tumour validation evidence.

**NSCLC:** Dato-DXd TROPION-Lung01 is the single highest-stakes post-AA ADC confirmatory in solid tumours within the knowledge base. It was approved via AA on ORR/DOR basis and now must show PFS/OS in the confirmatory setting. The MM/NSCLC analysts concur: if FDA applies Zynlonta-level post-AA scrutiny to solid-tumour ADC confirmatory trials, dato-DXd is the most exposed asset. Sacituzumab tirumotecan (sac-TMT) is a next-gen TROP2 competitor entering Phase 3 in both NSCLC and urothelial simultaneously — monitoring both confirmatory tracks is important.

**Urothelial:** The class story here is one of clear success followed by a confirmatory failure. EV + pembro (OS HR 0.47) is the class's best solid-tumour readout globally. Sac-gov's urothelial withdrawal (TROPiCS-04 2024) is the class's most recent confirmatory failure in a solid tumour before Zynlonta. The two failures together (urothelial sac-gov + DLBCL Zynlonta) bookend the AA-pathway risk narrative. Next-generation assets in urothelial (sac-TMT Phase 3, ASG-22ME-19 next-gen EV) are being designed with improved chemistry specifically to avoid TROPiCS-04-type OS failures. Disitamab vedotin HER2 franchise is China-origin (COINS Act watch) but clinically compelling.

**Gastric / GI (via CRC, PDAC cross-indication):** IBI343 CLDN18.2 ADC is the first validated GI ADC beyond HER2. This creates a new competitive axis: CLDN18.2 ADC vs mAb vs CAR-T in the GI CLDN18.2 segment. For CRC, expression is ~10-15% vs ~40-50% in gastric — the class validator does not automatically translate (HR-CRC-004). For PDAC, the stroma and lower surface density introduce an additional 0.6x probability discount vs gastric. The gastric win is commercially significant ($1.2B Takeda deal) but the PDAC/CRC ADC class read-across is inferential, not evidence-graded.

**CRC:** ADC pipeline is active but no Phase 3 confirmatory success in CRC-specific setting. HER2 ADC (T-DXd DESTINY-CRC02 ORR 45% IHC3+), CEACAM5 ADC (tusamitamab, M9140), EGFRxHER3 bispecific ADC (BL-B01D1) are all in Phase 2 or early Phase 3. The Zynlonta class signal has been flagged by CRC analyst for safety risk framing (HR-CRC-003) but no CRC-specific FDA action.

**PDAC:** No ADC-specific Phase 3 success. CLDN18.2 ADC read-across from gastric is the primary class signal (see above). Evidence coverage gap (all 9 PDAC events blank) limits evidence-graded PDAC ADC assessment — this remains a structural gap.

**AML/MDS:** Gemtuzumab ozogamicin (Mylotarg) is the class's historical hematologic precedent — CD33 calicheamicin with hepatotoxicity/VOD precedent. The AML analyst explicitly connects Zynlonta's confirmatory failure to the Mylotarg VOD precedent: any novel AML-directed ADC (CD33, CD123 targets) faces a dual precedent burden (Mylotarg toxicity class + Zynlonta confirmatory design class). Net: no active AML ADC in late-stage development; class risk makes the field cautious; the menin inhibitor class is the primary 2026-2028 investment focus in AML.

## Counter-Thesis — What Could Be Wrong About This Theme

**The Zynlonta precedent may be over-read:**

Loncastuximab tesirine (Zynlonta) is a PBD-dimer payload — a highly potent alkylating payload associated with vascular leak, pleural effusion, and severe hypersensitivity. The PBD-class toxicity profile is mechanistically distinct from DXd (topoisomerase I inhibitor) and MMAE (microtubule inhibitor). The precise death-rate signal may be payload-specific, not class-generic. If FDA's response is narrowly scoped to PBD-class hematologic ADCs (rather than all ADC confirmatory trials), the predicted class-wide scrutiny escalation would be overstated. Mirvetuximab's DM4 full approval (MIRASOL) and sac-gov's clean OS record in breast support the view that solid-tumour DXd/SN-38 ADCs are genuinely safer than PBD hematologic ADCs. The bifurcation narrative may be correct — but the degree of confirmatory trial bar elevation for solid-tumour DXd ADCs might be lower than the cross-indication signal currently implies.

**ADC competition may be from within (bispecific ADCs, conditional ADCs), not from other modalities:**

The next generation of competition for ADCs is not IO, small molecules, or CAR-T — it is more sophisticated ADC architectures: Probody conditional ADCs (Regeneron/CytomX), bispecific ADCs (BL-B01D1 EGFRxHER3), and ultra-high-DAR formats. If next-gen ADC chemistry resolves the confirmatory safety bar, the class narrative becomes one of intra-class evolution rather than inter-modality displacement. The two TROP2 ADCs (sac-gov vs dato-DXd) already demonstrate that payload/linker chemistry within one ADC target class determines clinical differentiation.

**In vivo cell therapy as long-horizon disruptor:**

Legend Biotech's in vivo lymphoma near-complete responses eliminate the manufacturing bottleneck that has historically constrained ex vivo ADC-competing modalities (CAR-T, bispecifics). If in vivo cell therapy achieves solid-tumour expansion (currently hematologic only) and targets overlap with ADC antigens (CD19, BCMA, HER2, TROP2), the premium placed on ADC as the "easiest to manufacture targeted cytotoxic" erodes. This is a 5-7 year horizon, not cycle-level, but the structural threat is real for haematologic ADCs specifically.

**China-origin ADC platform may survive COINS Act intact:**

The Innovent-Takeda $1.2B deal closed despite COINS Act/BIOSECURE escalation. If COINS Act legislation does not pass (proposed, not enacted as of Jun 5), China-origin ADC deals (IBI343, disitamab vedotin, any future Innovent/BeiGene ADC) face no formal regulatory barrier. The geopolitical risk is real but currently legislative rather than enacted — a meaningful probability exists that deals structured via US-incorporated partners (Summit Therapeutics for ivonescimab; Pfizer for disitamab vedotin) remain viable indefinitely.

## How This Theme Informs TPPs

A new asset entering the ADC class in 2026 faces the following differentiation requirements:

1. **Payload selection is binary reputationally:** DXd/SN-38 (topo-I) are the class benchmark payloads. PBD-class, calicheamicin, and maytansinoid ADCs face elevated scrutiny and require explicit safety differentiation. Any new ADC TPP should commit to topo-I class payload to avoid Zynlonta/Mylotarg/T-DM1 (DM1 off-target) class-by-association risk.

2. **Confirmatory trial design is now a first-class TPP requirement:** The Zynlonta signal means FDA will scrutinise confirmatory trial design at the IND stage. A TPP for an AA-seeking ADC must include OS as a co-primary or key secondary endpoint from Phase 2 onward, not just ORR/DOR. This is a development cost and timeline implication.

3. **Target selection must address expression heterogeneity and patient selection:** The sac-gov urothelial failure (vs sac-gov TNBC success) and dato-DXd OS failure in HR+/TNBC vs sac-gov TNBC success illustrate that even the same ADC can succeed or fail based on indication-specific biology. TPP must specify the patient selection strategy (biomarker expression threshold, line of therapy, histologic subtype) that maximises the probability of OS benefit.

4. **China-origin partnership risk must be disclosed in any TPP that references Innovent, BeiGene, RemeGen, or Gracell ADC platforms:** COINS Act legislative risk is now an explicit US market access factor in ADC partnership decisions.

5. **Next-gen differentiation angles with highest signal-to-noise:** Probody conditional activation (Regeneron/CytomX partnership), bispecific payload targeting (BL-B01D1 EGFRxHER3), ultra-high DAR (>8 DAR formats), and pH-responsive linker chemistry are the active next-gen design spaces with deal signal. An ADC TPP that does not specify differentiation from T-DXd or EV is not investor-ready in 2026.
